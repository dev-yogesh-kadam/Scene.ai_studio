# Lighting setups and moods

Light is the first thing a viewer feels and the last thing a prompt usually says. Name the light source, its
direction, its quality (hard or soft) and its colour; the model then builds a believable picture instead of a flat
average one.

## The words that matter
- **Source:** window, sun, streetlamp, candle, fridge light, phone screen, neon sign, fire, overhead fluorescent.
- **Direction:** front (flat, beauty), side (drama, texture), back / rim (separation, glow), top (harsh, interrogation),
  under (horror, campfire).
- **Quality:** hard (sharp shadows: midday sun, a bare bulb) or soft (gentle shadows: overcast, a big window, a diffuser).
- **Contrast:** high key (bright, few shadows: comedy, beauty, clean ads) or low key (deep shadows: drama, thriller,
  luxury).
- **Colour temperature:** warm (2700–3500 K: tungsten, candle, golden hour) or cool (5600 K+ daylight, blue hour,
  moonlight). Mixed warm and cool in one frame gives depth.

## Setups that work
| Setup | Words for the prompt | Mood | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Window light | soft daylight from a large window on the left, gentle falloff | honest, calm, intimate | portraits, cafe, food, testimonials |
| Golden hour | low warm sun from behind, long shadows, glowing rim on hair | nostalgia, romance, hope | brand films, romance, endings |
| Blue hour | deep blue dusk sky, warm windows lit, street lamps coming on | quiet, wistful | cityscapes, transitions |
| Rembrandt | single soft key from 45° above, a small triangle of light on the far cheek | classic, serious | drama, portraits |
| Rim + dark ground | strong backlight outlining the subject against black | premium, mysterious | product hero, luxury, music |
| Practical-lit night | lit only by the lamps, screens and signs in the scene | real, moody, cinematic | night scenes, street, interiors |
| Neon night | magenta and cyan signs reflecting on wet ground | energy, urban, youth | chases, music video, nightlife |
| High-key studio | bright soft even light, clean white ground | clean, modern, trustworthy | product, explainer, beauty |
| Hard noon | harsh overhead sun, short black shadows | heat, tension, realism | westerns, documentary, conflict |
| Firelight / candle | warm flickering light from below one side | warmth or menace | stories, period, horror |

## Mood → light, quickly
- Comfort, home: warm, soft, low, practical lamps.
- Tension: hard side light, deep shadows, one cool source.
- Romance: warm backlight, soft key, haze.
- Fear: under-lighting or a single source far away; darkness the eye must search.
- Joy: bright, high key, colour.
- Luxury: dark ground, controlled rim light, reflections.

## Continuity of light
- One scene, one light: repeat the same light sentence word for word in every shot of it (see `screen-direction`).
- Motivate changes: a door opens and light floods in; the sun goes behind a cloud.

## In the studio
- Put the light in the prompt's LIGHT AND LOOK section (or where the model's prompt guide wants it).
- Night scenes: name the practical sources or the model invents random neon.
- Presets (`preset_search`, category lighting) hold tested light words; stack one key setup with at most one look.

## Traps
- "Cinematic lighting" with no source: the model guesses and averages.
- Neon in a 1950s village or a quiet home (see the director's era rule: light only from real sources of that world).
- Flat front light on a dramatic scene.
- A new light direction in each shot of one scene.
