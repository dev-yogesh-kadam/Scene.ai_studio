# Aspect ratio and platform specs

Shape is a creative decision before it is a technical one: it decides how the viewer holds the phone, how big a face
is and what the frame can hold. Decide it from the brief (the platform) and keep it for every shot of the piece.

## Shapes and where they go
| Shape | Studio orientation | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | portrait | Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Stories, WhatsApp status | full-screen phone; faces and single subjects |
| 4:5 | portrait (crop) | Instagram and Facebook feed | the most feed space; crop from 9:16 or reframe |
| 1:1 | square | feed posts, product tiles, covers, carousels | safe everywhere; centred compositions |
| 16:9 | landscape | YouTube, TV, website, presentations, cinema | groups, landscapes, wide action |
| 21:9 | landscape (crop) | cinematic look, banners | letterbox inside 16:9 |

## Safe areas (keep faces, products and type inside them)
- **Reels / TikTok / Shorts (9:16):** keep the top ~14% and the bottom ~20–25% clear of anything important: the
  platform's name, captions, buttons and description sit there; also leave ~10% clear on the right for the like and
  share buttons. The subject lives in the middle 4:5 area.
- **Stories:** keep the top and bottom ~14% (about 250 px of 1920) clear.
- **YouTube (16:9):** keep titles inside 90% of the frame; the end screen's last 5–20 s may be covered by its cards.
- **Feed 4:5 and 1:1:** a little margin on every side for the interface; the first frame is the thumbnail.

## Lengths that work (the platforms allow more)
| Platform | Sweet spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reels / TikTok | 7–30 s | loop-friendly; hook in 1 s |
| Shorts | 15–45 s | at most 60 s to count as a Short |
| Stories | 5–15 s a card | a sequence of short cards |
| Feed video | 15–60 s | captions burned in; sound off by default |
| YouTube pre-roll | 6 s (bumper) or 15–30 s | the first 5 s before "Skip" carry the brand |
| TV / cinema spot | 15, 30 or 60 s exactly | |
| Brand film on web | 60–120 s | |

## Sound
- Feeds play muted: the piece must work without sound; burn captions in (the film tool's subtitles) when words matter.
- Reels and TikTok are watched with sound more often: music choice and a beat-synced cut matter there.

## Designing for the shape
- **Portrait:** one subject, vertical movement (rising, falling, walking toward camera), faces close; landscapes become
  details.
- **Square:** centred, symmetrical, graphic.
- **Landscape:** groups, horizons, lateral movement, wide establishing shots.
- One piece for several platforms: shoot (generate) for the tallest shape the brief needs and reframe for the others,
  keeping the subject central — or plan a portrait and a landscape version as separate passes. The film tool's reframe
  crops to a window; outpaint extends (it invents what was never filmed; small changes look best).

## In the studio
- `orientation` portrait, landscape or square; an image model's aspect ratio choice where it has one.
- All shots of one piece keep one shape; decide it in the first step of a chain.
- 1080 resolution for a final shot of 10 s or less; a draft at 480.

## Traps
- A landscape ad posted to Reels with black bars.
- Captions at the very bottom of a 9:16 frame, under the platform's buttons.
- A 90-second piece for TikTok with the idea at second 40.
