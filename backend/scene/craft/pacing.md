# Pacing and editing to music

Pacing is how time feels. Short shots compress time and raise energy; long shots stretch it and add weight. A piece
with every shot the same length has no rhythm, however good each shot is.

## Shot length by purpose
| Purpose | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook, impact, reaction | 1.5–3 s | quick read, energy |
| Action beat (a jump, a pour) | 3–5 s | the action's real time |
| Dialogue line | words ÷ 2.5 + 2 s | the studio's speaking pace, a beat before and after |
| Establishing / place | 4–7 s | the eye needs time to read a wide |
| Emotional hold | 5–9 s | let the feeling land |
| Product hero | 3–5 s | long enough to want it, short enough not to sell it twice |
In the studio a clip is at least about 4 s; for shorter beats, plan the shot and cut it in the assembly (trim), or use
the film tool's beat cut.

## Rhythm
- **Accelerate into the peak:** 5 s → 4 s → 3 s → 2 s, then hold on the payoff. The audience feels the build.
- **Contrast:** after a run of fast cuts, one long shot hits hard. After a long hold, a quick cut wakes them up.
- **Breathe:** leave a beat after the most important moment before the next thing starts.
- **End decisively:** the last shot is held slightly longer, or cut on a final beat to black or the end card.

## Cutting to music
- Find the tempo (BPM): at 120 BPM a beat is 0.5 s, a bar of four is 2 s. Shot lengths of 2, 4 or 8 beats feel right.
- **Cut on the beat** for energy; **cut on the downbeat** (the first beat of a bar) for structure; cut **just before**
  the beat for urgency.
- Match the music's sections: calm verse → longer shots; chorus → faster cuts and the strongest images; the drop →
  the hook image or the reveal.
- A sound or musical hit on a visual hit (a door slam, a jump landing, the logo) doubles the impact.
- Silence before a drop or a reveal makes it land.
- The film tool's `beats` op gives the tempo, beats and bars; `beat_cut` cuts clips on them.

## Pacing by format
- **Reels / Shorts / TikTok:** a change every 1.5–3 s; the hook in the first second; total 7–30 s.
- **Ad (15–30 s):** one idea, 5–10 shots, the payoff by second 10–20, the end card 2–3 s.
- **Brand film (60–90 s):** slower, 4–8 s shots, one musical arc, a single emotional peak.
- **Music video:** cut to the song's structure; repeat visual motifs on repeated sections.
- **Drama scene:** pace follows the performance; hold reactions, cut on looks.
- **Explainer:** one idea per 3–5 s; type on screen long enough to read twice.

## The studio's assembly
- `assemble` joins this plan's shots in story order with music, a title and an end card. Plan the music length as the
  shots added up (the music step's `duration`).
- Plan durations from the action, not round numbers (see "Settings" in the rules), and make the total fit the platform.

## Traps
- Every shot 5 s because that is the default.
- Cutting on every beat for 60 seconds (exhausting) — vary between every beat, every 2 and every 4.
- A slow intro on a social reel.
- An end card that arrives after the music has ended.
