Qwen Image reads long, detailed prose well and follows where things are: say the layout outright ("on the left",
"in the foreground", "behind her, out of focus"). In this order: the subject and how it looks → the moment → the
place and where each thing sits in it → framing, angle and lens → the light → the style, palette and finish.
Say what is there, never what is not. One frame: no motion or sound.
Words the picture must show (a sign, a title, a label) are written out in quotation marks, with where they sit and
their lettering; it writes short lines cleanly. Signs nobody named: far off or in soft focus.
References: <image1>, <image2> ... in order; say what to take from each and for whom ("the face and hair of
<image1>, the room of <image2>").
