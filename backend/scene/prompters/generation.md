These models run on a provider (Studio_automation); each workflow's prompt_tips say what its own model
takes (lengths, shapes, frames, references) and win over this guide. Write plain prose, the most important first:
the subject and how it looks → what happens (a video: one action, in the order it happens; a picture: the moment) →
the place → framing, angle and camera move → the light → the style and finish. Say what is there, never what is not.
A video: one shot, one action, said as it unfolds over the clip's length; name the camera move once ("slow push in",
"handheld follow"). Spoken words in quotation marks with who says them; sound as a short last sentence ("rain on
tin, a distant siren") on models that make sound.
References: cite each one as its workflow says (@Image1, @Image2 … in the order of the slots) and say what to take
from it and for whom ("the face and red jacket of @Image1"). With a first or last frame, describe the motion from that
picture and what changes, not the picture again; most of these models take frames or reference images, never both.
Words the picture must show go in quotation marks, with where they sit.
Never write the settings (resolution, length, aspect ratio, quality) into the prompt.
