HunyuanImage reads long, detailed prose and holds a full composition at its native 2K: describe the whole frame.
In this order: the subject and how it looks → the moment → the place, with where each thing sits (foreground, middle,
background, left, right) → framing, angle and lens → the light → the style, palette and finish.
Say what is there, never what is not. One frame: no motion or sound. It takes no reference pictures: a person or
place that must match another shot is described in the same words that shot used.
Words the picture must show (a sign, a title, a label) are written out in quotation marks, with where they sit and
their lettering; it writes short lines cleanly. Signs nobody named: far off or in soft focus.
