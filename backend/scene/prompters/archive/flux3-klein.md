This workflow's prompt upsampler expands the prompt before the model reads it. Give it the facts that must survive,
plainly and in full, and let it add the detail: no lists of quality words ("masterpiece, 8k, ultra detailed").
In this order: the subject and how it looks → what it is doing → the place → framing and angle → the light → the
style. The same rules as FLUX.2: say what is there, never what is not; one frame, no motion or sound; words the
picture must show written out in quotation marks with where they sit and their lettering; other signs and shop boards
far off or in soft focus, since letters nobody named come out garbled.
