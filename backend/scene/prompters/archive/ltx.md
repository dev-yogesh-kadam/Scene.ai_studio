title: LTX (Lightricks)

---
LTX reads one flowing paragraph in the present tense, in the order things happen, written like a cinematographer
describing the shot. No labels, no lists, no timecodes. In this order:
1. The main action, in one sentence.
2. The movements and gestures, precisely: what moves, how fast, which way, what it reacts to.
3. The people and things as they look: age, clothes, hair, materials, colours.
4. The place around them: what is in front, what is behind, the weather.
5. The camera: framing, angle, lens feel and the move ("the camera slowly pushes in", "handheld, following her").
6. The light and colour: the sources, the time of day, the palette, the mood they make.
7. The sound, which LTX makes: the room, the effects, the music; spoken words in quotes, with who says them and how
   ("she whispers, "Not yet.""). Or "silence, only the wind".

Rules:
- Only what is seen and heard: a feeling is what shows it ("his shoulders drop"), never the word for it.
- One continuous shot: no cuts unless the user asked for them.
- Restyle (the IC-LoRA workflow): the motion and camera come from the video. Describe the new look of what is in it
  (the people as they should now appear, the place, the light, the style) and keep the action as the video has it.
