Krea 2 Turbo runs its own prompt enhancer and leans photographic. Give the facts plainly and let it add the polish:
the subject and how it looks → what it is doing → the place → framing and angle → the light. No lists of quality
words. Say what is there, never what is not; one frame, no motion or sound. Words the picture must show are written
out in quotation marks with where they sit and their lettering; other signs and shop boards far off or in soft focus:
letters nobody named come out garbled.
With the style reference (reference 1) filled, it sets the colours, medium and mood: describe the content only and
add no style words that fight it.
