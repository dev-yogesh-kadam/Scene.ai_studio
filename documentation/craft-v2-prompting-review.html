Craft v2 Prompting Review

The prompting in craft skills v2/ compared with the studio's own prompting, for every picture workflow (6) and every video workflow (11) in workflows/. On v2's side that means its four video prompters and the picture prompts written inside asset-designer, anchor and script-to-video. On ours, the eight model guides in backend/scene/prompters/, each workflow's hints, the agent's prompt rules and our asset-designer.

01Summary

  • v2 has four video prompters: MiniMax H3, Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.5 and Wan 3.0. Only H3 runs in the studio. v2 has no picture prompter. Its picture prompts are four templates inside other skills (character sheet, location frame, anchor, shot frame), all written for Seedream 5 Pro.
  • The studio has eight guides, one per model family. Together they cover 15 of our 17 workflows. Talking Head has no guide and falls back to the general structure. Lip Sync takes no prompt.
  • H3 is the one real overlap (three workflows). Both sides use the same eight blocks. v2 is better at wiring: a role line for every slot, the order to drop references in, voice and audio locks, continuity lines and a report after each call. Ours is better at how H3 behaves in our own renders: one action takes 1–2 beats, the action stays in frame, a new framing is a hard cut, and the word limits are enforced.
  • For pictures, v2's templates have the better content (sheets, anchors). On our models they hit four problems:
    • their "No …" words, while our FLUX guide says to write no negatives;
    • the 200-word limit on FLUX.3 Klein and Krea 2 (a filled sheet prompt is about 230 words);
    • four different ways of naming references;
    • HunyuanImage (no references) and Krea 2 (a style reference only), which can't make family sheets, changed looks or anchors.
  • Our asset-designer has already lost v2 words: 6 phrases from the sheet prompt and 12 from the location prompt. That breaks your rule to keep every v2 word.
  • LTX and Wan 2.x have no v2 counterpart. Wan 3's layout doesn't suit Wan 2.x, which needs a short prompt and makes no sound. Five general lines from the Seedance and Wan 3 prompters would still help our guides.
  • Nine decisions are at the end (P1–P9). They follow on from D1–D16 in the first v2 review, which still stand.

02Who covers what

v2's prompting

  • craft-prompter-minimax-h3: 8 steps from inputs to the call, 8 blocks.
  • craft-prompter-seedance-2 and -2-5: 8 blocks, plus edit and extend modes.
  • craft-prompter-wan-3: Alibaba's five sections in square brackets.
  • Picture templates: the sheet and location frame (craft-asset-designer §2), the anchor (craft-anchor §4) and the shot frame (script-to-video step 8), all for Seedream 5 Pro.
  • Rules spread over the director: no readable text, light only from real sources, feelings shown as a visible tell.

The studio's prompting

  • 8 guides: flux2, flux3-klein, hunyuan-image, krea2, qwen-image, minimax-h3, ltx, wan. Each has a target length and a word limit, and a prompt over the limit goes back to the agent.
  • Each workflow's hints and pick_when (H3 Chunk has 10 hints).
  • The agent's rules: one action per shot, prompts in English with spoken words in the user's language, no settings or tags in a prompt, "a complete shot", and the general structure (SCENE CONTEXT … AUDIO) for workflows with no guide.
  • Camera fields turned into words (camera.apply).
  • The sheet and location prompts in our asset-designer (from v1, trimmed).

H3 15 s (720p)

This workflow takes three prompts (seconds 0–5, 5–10, 10–15), and each part starts where the last ended. Only part 1's field counts as "the prompt", so the guide and its limit are written for one prompt. Parts 2 and 3 are plain text fields, guided only by the hint "repeat the same scene description in all three prompts and change only the one small action and the line of each part". v2 has nothing like it. Its nearest rule is text continuity: each part's OPENING restates the last part's END STATE. See P6.

05Other video workflows

Talking Head / Narration No guide

The guide pattern ^video/h3_ misses this workflow, so it gets the general structure, whose AUDIO section asks for the spoken words. The workflow's own field says the opposite: Describe the delivery, motion and camera (not the words). Call the portrait <Picture 1>.

From v2: frames mode (motion and camera only), recorded audio lips syncing to it word for word, and use the attached audio strictly as-is. From ours: the camera keeps the portrait's framing. See P7.

Lip Sync No prompt

A video and a voice track, nothing written. Nothing to compare.

LTX-2.5 Ours only

Our ltx guide: one flowing present-tense paragraph, 100–200 words, sound included. v2 has no LTX prompter. The rules both sides share already apply: feelings as what shows, and no settings in the prompt. The workflow itself has been broken since October (blurred blobs) and needs fixing on Zoro.

Restyle Video: LTX-2.3 Ours only

Our guide's restyle line: describe the new look and keep the action as the video has it. The nearest v2 lines are Wan 3's Replace <subject> in @Video1 with <name>, keeping the action, path, and scene unchanged and Seedance's "the new object inherits the timing, path, speed and occlusion". The workflow has a fixed English negative prompt of its own.

Restyle: Wan VACE and Wan 2.2 Fun Control Ours only

Our wan guide: 60–120 words of plain prose (the umT5 encoder loses the end of a long prompt), the new look only, never a different action. Both workflows have a fixed Chinese negative prompt the agent can't change, which does the job of Wan 3's [Negative Prompts] section. Wan 3's partial-borrowing line fits VACE when its reference is a style picture rather than a restyled first frame.

Change shape: Wan VACE Ours only

Our guide: describe what lies around the frame as a still place, with nothing new that moves and no new people. The nearest v2 line is the extend-mode lock each subject stays one continuous instance — no duplicates, which would fit this workflow's joins of 5-second parts.

Wan Animate 2 Guide vs labels

Our guide says: describe the background, the light on the character and the camera, not the character's look or its moves. The workflow's two fields say Describe the character and the background and Describe the motion and the camera, and the guide never mentions the second field. v2 has nothing for character animation. See P8.

07Our own gaps, found on the way

These are in the studio whether or not v2 is ported.

  1. Talking Head has no guide, because the pattern ^video/h3_ misses it.
  2. H3 15 s: the guide covers part 1's field only.
  3. H3 All in One's two keyframe prompts (Qwen Image Edit, fields 405 and 406) have no guide on either side.
  4. Wan Animate 2: the guide and the field labels disagree, and the second field isn't mentioned.
  5. HunyuanImage and Qwen Image show a NEGATIVE PROMPT field (101) that no guide mentions.
  6. Speech is written in three ways: says: "…" in the guide, after Audio: in the agent's rule and in H3 All in One's hint.
  7. On H3 Chunk, script-to-video step 6 can fill both the voice sample and the voice track. v2 says never both.
  8. Our asset-designer doesn't name a picture workflow for sheets, so a sheet can land on a model whose limit sends it back.
  9. LTX-2.5 text to video has been broken since October.

08Decisions

Each one either changes v2's words or changes what the studio does today, so each needs your answer. D7, D10 and D16 from the first review still stand.

P1Bring back v2's dropped words

Our asset-designer lost 18 phrases from the sheet and location prompts, plus the palette and style lines (§03).

Recommend restore them word for word, as your rule says. P5 decides where the "No …" words go.

P2Add v2's missing H3 lines to our guide

Several views make one person, the motion video line, no clip frames as identity, the drop order with anchors, the as-is audio lock, no voice sample next to a voice track, drop a feeling with no tell, and the report. Plus one line for planned cuts: inside a skill run, each planned shot after the first opens with "Cut to" and gets its keyframe.

Recommend yes. I'm asking because the guide applies to every H3 prompt, not just skill runs. D10 (the H3 prompter as a skill, all its text kept) stands either way.

P3One way to write speech

<name> says: "…" (v2 and our guide) or after Audio: (the agent's rule and H3 All in One's hint).

Recommend says: everywhere, and change the agent's rule and the hint to match.

P4Which picture model gets v2's templates

Sheets and anchors need references, the words kept as written, and room for about 230 words.

Recommend FLUX.2 dev; Qwen only for unpaid work; HunyuanImage only for sheets without references; never FLUX.3 Klein or Krea 2. Name the model in asset-designer and anchor. That is a model swap only, plus the FLUX.3 numbering note if it is ever used.

P5v2's "No …" words

v2's sheets use them, its anchor bans them, and our FLUX guide bans them.

Recommend keep every word. On Qwen and HunyuanImage, put the "No …" phrases in the NEGATIVE PROMPT field. On FLUX.2, keep them in the prompt and first run one sheet with them and one without through scripts/prompt_ab.py.

P6H3 15 s: three prompts

Nothing says what goes in parts 2 and 3.

Recommend one guide line: GOAL, SCENE, STYLE and LOCKS repeat word for word in all three parts; each part's OPENING is the last part's END STATE; only SHOTS change. This is v2's text-continuity rule.

P7A Talking Head guide

Today it falls back to a structure that asks for the spoken words.

Recommend a new prompters/talking-head.md built from v2's frames-mode and recorded-audio lines plus our two hints (describe the delivery, keep the portrait's framing).

P8Wan Animate 2: describe the character or not

The guide says no; the field labels say yes.

Recommend render both versions on Zoro, then fix whichever is wrong: the guide or the labels.

P9Borrow five general lines

The Seedance and Wan 3 lines in §06: name after every tag, screen side and a visible trait, one camera move, no tag for an absent subject, partial borrowing.

Recommend add them to our H3 guide, plus partial borrowing to the Krea 2 and Wan guides. Nothing else from those prompters.
