The cast's reference sheets

asset-designer

A list of every recurring person, place and prop first, then "Do you have references?". Each sheet goes into NeuralCore under its name. Families are made oldest first. Changed looks become "Maya - torn sleeve", and night scenes "Kitchen - night".

Runs on: an image workflow that takes reference pictures works now · Skipped for a detailed script.
4

Directing

director+ scene presets

Each scene gets its idea ("This scene is shot as…"), then the beats and the turn. Every shot gets a lens, angle and move with a reason. Fight, romance, suspense and the other scene types bring their own craft. Clips are cut to H3's 15 s.

A detailed script stops here, written as prose.

3 · Helpers

The purple skills, and who calls them

asset-designer

Reference sheets for a whole script's cast, places and props, saved into NeuralCore.

Called by script-to-video (step 3), or /asset-designer
director

Turns a script into scenes and shots, with a reason for every camera choice. Uses the 14 scene presets.

Called by script-to-video (step 4), or /director
continuity-chain

Each clip starts where the last one ended.

Called by script-to-video when you pick Chain
continuity-board

A numbered grid of stills from the scene so far guides each new clip.

Called by script-to-video when you pick Board
continuity-recall

A library of labelled keyframes, so someone who comes back looks the same as before.

Called by script-to-video when you pick Recall
camera-coverage

Only with /camera-coverage

Only with /sound-pass
4 · Continuity

Chain, Board or Recall?

continuity-chain

Chain (the default)

Pick it when one scene must flow without a break.

Each clip starts on the last frame of the one before. Redoing one clip means redoing every later clip in that scene.

works now, from the last frame
true extension: video_extend_wan_vace
continuity-board

Board

Pick it when you want to redo any clip on its own.

Every clip is made fresh. A grid of up to 6 numbered stills shows the scene so far, and its highest number is where the clip starts.

works now
continuity-recall

Recall

Pick it when people, places or props come back much later.

Every kept shot leaves a labelled keyframe. The next clip gets only the few it needs, in one grid. It can also run beside Chain or Board.

works now

What lights up when the Zoro workflows are connected

Zoro has 10 tested workflows, but their files are still only on Zoro. Once they're copied over and connected:

  • Chain gets true extension (video_extend_wan_vace).
  • Two people speak in one clip (video_multitalk).
  • Voices come from text (audio_tts_clone).
  • Sound effects (audio_sfx_from_text).
  • 1080p and 4K (video_upscale_seedvr2).
