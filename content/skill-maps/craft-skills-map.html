Found while reading

Loose ends in the links

Three guides are named but not in the folder.The Wan 3 and H3 prompters say to load video-guide-wan and video-guide-minimax first. Board and recall point to ffmpeg-recipes for numbering and tiling.
Names hold the links together.The director's cast line ("Maya - torn sleeve · Kitchen - night") is turned into asset-designer's paths by script-to-video. If a name is spelt differently, the prompter gets no sheet.
Chain says it works anywhere, but script-to-video allows it on two models only.continuity-chain says "works inside any workflow". script-to-video runs it only on Seedance 2.5 and Wan 3, and turns it into Board elsewhere.
Some prompter inputs have no skill that fills them.retake and copyright_map come only from the user. The director's voiceover reaches the prompters inside the shots, since no prompter has a voiceover field.
