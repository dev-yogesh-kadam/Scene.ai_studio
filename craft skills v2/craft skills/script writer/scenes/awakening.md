# Power awakening / transformation
Use when a character's power or nature first shows itself: the weak one's hidden power breaks out, a system awakens, a bloodline lights up, a curse or a bite takes hold, someone learns to control what they have. Mix with `humiliation` when the power answers a shaming, `trial` for an assessment, `fight` when it is used, `horror` when it is monstrous, `triumph` when it is a win.

## What the scene is really about
- A body finding something it can do, or becoming something it wasn't. Wonder, fear and pain at once.
- The rule and the cost: what triggers it, what it takes, what it can't do. Shown once, plainly.
- Who sees it. An awakening alone is a secret; one before witnesses changes the hero's place in the world.

## Pick the engine
- **The breaking point:** pushed to the edge (cornered, shamed, someone they love in danger), the power answers.
- **The slow rise:** a sign, a bigger sign, then the full thing, in stages across the scene.
- **The test:** a formal measure (a crystal, a gauge, an elder's hand) shows it, and the room reacts.
- **The accident:** it breaks out beyond the hero's control, and something gets broken.
- **The transformation:** the body changes, and the person inside fights it or welcomes it.
- **Calibration:** the hero learns to control it, failing first.

## Staging and blocking
- Before: the hero at their weakest, in the place the power will change.
- The power has one look, fixed in the bible (its colour, its shape, where it shows on the body), the same every time.
- It starts from one place on the body: the eyes, a palm, a mark on the chest.
- Let it touch the world: dust lifting, stone cracking, light thrown across faces, people stepping back.
- Witnesses give it scale: how far they step back, who kneels, who runs.

## Performance
- It hurts or overwhelms first: gasping, shaking, a hand pressed to the ground. Then control, or not.
- The face carries what it means: fear, wonder, rage, relief. One honest reaction.
- Witnesses react in character: the rival afraid, the master proud, the crowd silent.

## The turn
- The power shows in full and changes the situation: the enemy thrown back, the gauge shattered, the room on its knees.
- Put the hero at their lowest just before it.

## After the turn / the end
- The cost: blood from the nose, the light gone out, the body spent, the people now afraid.
- End on the hero's face seeing what they are.

## Traps
- A power with no rule or cost.
- The awakening off-screen or lost in the dark.
- A different colour or shape each time it appears.
- No witnesses, when the story needs the hero's place to change.
- Effects stacked until the face is lost.
- A long victory pose after it.
